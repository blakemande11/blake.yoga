Greetings

I'm Blake, a yogi, meditator, and human originally from New York. In August 2024, I left my corporate consulting job in NYC to follow a deeper calling. Now based in Auckland after enriching stays in Hawaii and Australia, I've dedicated myself to teaching and practicing yoga. Throughout my journey, yoga has been my anchor — an incredible tool that continually brings me back to myself and helps me appreciate life's gradual unfolding.

I first came to yoga while in university, building a consistent home practice during the 2020 pandemic before completing a teacher training in Bali with Yoga East + West. From then, my practice has been shaped by the many great teachers I worked with in NYC at St. Mark's Yoga, the teachings of vipassana meditation, zen buddhism, and wherever else my curiosity takes me.

I truly believe yoga is for everybody — that the practice has a magical way of meeting you where you're at. I feel grateful that I get to share my experience of yoga with others and hope to practice with you soon!
